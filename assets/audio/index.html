    Sounds¶

    The shared version 2 manifest also supports lua.v1. A mixed pack declares both assets.audio.v1 and lua.v1; its script starts after the audio files finish loading. See the Lua guide for script activation and achievement rules.

    A mod can replace two of the game's own sound signals with sounds of its own: confirm and research. Sounds are WAV or OGG files declared in the mod's mod.json with formatVersion 2.

    Two small example mods show the whole thing: example_audio.zip (a bell for the research signal, as a WAV file) and example_audio_ogg.zip (a tick for the confirm signal, as an OGG Vorbis file).

    A mod with a sound¶

    {
  "format": "car-line-tycoon.mod",
  "formatVersion": 2,
  "gameVersion": ">=0.6.4",
  "id": "harbour_audio",
  "version": "1.0.0",
  "name": { "en": "Harbour Audio", "it": "Suoni del porto" },
  "authors": ["Example Author"],
  "type": "pack",
  "requires": ["assets.audio.v1"],
  "assets": [
    {
      "id": "research_bell",
      "kind": "audio",
      "path": "audio/research_bell.wav",
      "license": "CC0-1.0",
      "author": "Example Author"
    }
  ],
  "audioBindings": [
    { "cue": "research", "asset": "research_bell", "gainDb": -3 }
  ]
}

    The folder looks like this:

    Mods/
  harbour_audio/
    mod.json
    audio/
      research_bell.wav

    The fields are described on the Manifest page. In short:

    • requires lists the capabilities the mod needs. A sound mod requires assets.audio.v1.
    • assets declares every file. Each asset has an id, the kind audio, a path inside the mod folder, and the license and author of the file. A licenseFile with the licence text is optional.
    • audioBindings says which asset plays for which signal, with a gainDb between -24 and 0.
    • The type of a mod with assets is pack. A pack may also hold any of the five JSON file types.

    A pack needs at least one thing the game can use: a JSON file, or a sound with a binding. A pack with only sounds works without any JSON file.

    The signals¶

    Signal cue When it plays
    Confirm confirm When you confirm an action with the game's confirm buttons.
    Research completed research When a research topic finishes. It plays once for each completed topic. Starting or cancelling a research makes no sound.

    A mod binds at most one sound to a signal and at most two signals in all. When the mod is off, or the sound cannot be loaded, the signal keeps the game's own sound.

    Sound files¶

    Allowed
    Formats WAV (RIFF, PCM, 16 bit, little-endian) and OGG with Vorbis audio in one stream
    Not accepted Opus, WAV with float samples or ADPCM, RF64, OGG files that hold several streams
    Sample rate 22050, 44100 or 48000 Hz
    Channels 1 (mono) or 2 (stereo)
    Length From 0.05 to 5 seconds
    File size 2 MiB at most
    Decoded size The samples of the bound sounds of a mod, as 32-bit numbers, take at most 8 MiB
    Paths Lower-case letters, digits, _, - and .; folders separated by /; at most four parts and 128 characters; the extension is .wav or .ogg

    Other limits of a mod: 256 files in the mod folder (three folder levels deep), 64 assets, 2 audio bindings, a manifest of 256 KiB at most, nested at most 16 levels, with no text over 4096 characters and no property written twice. The active mods together can declare 512 MiB of files, and at most 32 mods with assets are used; the ones after that, in the order of their ids, stay off and say so.

    The game reads each declared file once, through a confined reader: no absolute path, no .., no drive or stream names, no reserved Windows names, and no link or junction on the way.

    Loudness¶

    Prepare your sounds around -18 LUFS for effects with a true peak under -1 dBTP. The game measures the sample peak and the RMS level of every sound when it loads it, and lowers a sound that is louder than -1 dBFS at the peak or -18 dBFS RMS. It never raises a quiet sound. Then it applies your gainDb, from -24 to 0.

    When the game lowers a sound, the Mods page lists a note with the amount. The sound itself is not changed on disk.

    Listening and using the sounds¶

    On the Mods page, a mod with sounds has one Listen button for each signal it binds. The button plays the sound at your sound-effects volume. It follows the mute and the game window: nothing plays while the game is muted or the window has no focus. You can listen to a sound of a mod that is switched off.

    The sounds of the mods that are switched on are used by the games you start or load after the change.

    Two mods, one signal¶

    When two active mods bind the same signal, both stay valid. The signal uses the game's own sound, and both mods show a note saying which signal is shared. Signals that only one of them binds keep playing their sound. Switch one mod off and the other one plays.

    Errors and notes on the Mods page¶

    A mod with a problem has a red row. The row shows the first error: the file, a code, the value the game found and the limit. Details opens the list of up to 20 errors and notes. A sound mod with one error is not loaded at all; no part of it plays.

    Code Meaning
    asset_path The path is not allowed, the file is missing, a folder is in the way or a link was found.
    asset_format The kind of file is not accepted: another extension, Opus, float WAV, 8-bit, a sample rate or channel count outside the list, or an OGG with several streams.
    asset_budget A size or count limit was passed: file size, length, number of files, assets or bindings, decoded size, mods with assets.
    asset_decode The file is damaged or incomplete: a truncated file, a chunk or page that ends outside the file, a bad checksum, or a decoder that failed when the sound was loaded.
    asset_conflict Two mods bind the same signal, or two mod folders have the same id.
    asset_binding A binding or asset declaration is wrong: an unknown signal or asset, a signal bound twice, an id used twice, a gain outside -24 to 0, or assets in a mod that is not a pack.
    asset_license An asset has no license or no author.
    asset_capability The mod uses assets without listing assets.audio.v1 in requires. A mod that requires a capability this game does not have gets its own warning that names the capability.
    asset_normalized A note: the game lowered a sound that was too loud.
    asset_changed The file changed between the game's check and its loading. Press Reload.

    Saves and removed mods¶

    A save lists the mods that were active in it, with the hash of their files. When you remove a sound mod later, the save loads as before, the game keeps showing that it was played with mods, and the signals return to the game's own sounds. Sounds do not change the economy or the achievements of a game.
